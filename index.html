HOW IT WORKS

One student answers. Everyone gets the answer.

Instead of every student having to search for the exact same questions over and over, the toolkit connects everyone on Moodle to a shared answer pool. When anyone finishes and reviews a quiz, verified answers are saved automatically. The next time you—or anyone else—opens that quiz, the answer is already highlighted and ready in one click.

Shared Answer Pool

Answers are saved across quizzes. If another student has already solved the question, the verified answer appears for you immediately.

100% Anonymous Sharing

When you review a completed quiz, verified checkmarks are contributed automatically. Zero personal names, student IDs, or logins are ever collected.

1-Click Solving & Shortcuts

Auto-highlights correct choices on Moodle, with instant shortcuts like C to copy for AI, V to paste AI answers, and 1-click lecture completion.
