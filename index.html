ABOUT THE TOOLKIT

Intelligent Study Companion & Crowdsourced Answer Network

AMAES Toolkit is an open-source assistive companion designed specifically for students of AMA Online Education (AMAOEd / AMAES) and ACLC College. It eliminates study friction by pairing smart real-time keyword matching with a massive, crowd-powered verified answer repository.

Massive Keyword & Answer Database

Connects directly to thousands of verified question keys across all courses. The built-in solver engine uses 5-tier fuzzy keyword matching to instantly pinpoint verified solutions and strike out debunked choices.

100% Anonymous Answer Sharing

Every time you review a completed quiz, verified checkmarks and 100% full-mark answers are automatically contributed to the collective question pool. Zero student IDs, names, cookies, or account tokens are ever collected or sent.

Autonomous Co-Pilot & AI Clipboard

Navigate quizzes with smart auto-selection and single-letter hotkeys. Copy questions formatted cleanly for AI with C, paste AI responses directly to form controls with V, and batch-complete lectures with a single click.
